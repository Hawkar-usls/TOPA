# PASS-002 source note

Public source checked before raw-data inspection:

- Project Hessdalen — Current activities / Stream Stacker.

It documents two Dahua N85EFN2 cameras on the Blue Box mast, 4K at 25 fps, west/south views, 20-minute segments, approximately 45 GB per camera/day, public stacked images since September 2024, raw video dating back to November 2024, and a full day of videos available for testing.

It also explicitly warns about ordinary classes and processing artifacts including stars, satellites, birds, insects, aircraft, bright-object H.264 shadows and internal lens reflections.

These facts are treated as experiment metadata and known-artifact priors, not as anomaly classifications.
