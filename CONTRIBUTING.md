# Contributing to TOPA

TOPA welcomes skeptical reviews, ordinary explanations, corrections, negative results, source recovery and genuinely anomalous cases.

Agreement with an existing TOPA interpretation is not required.

## Minimum case envelope

A useful submission should contain, when available:

```text
claim_id
raw claim/report
source pointer(s)
provenance channel
firsthand vs hearsay status
time + time standard
location
what was observed
what was inferred
at least one competing explanation
at least one test that could lower confidence
current bounded status
```

## Preferred evidence

Prefer primary or near-primary material:

- original records;
- instrument data;
- contemporaneous logs;
- archival documents;
- exact timestamps and time standards;
- coordinates and geometry;
- independent source chains;
- reproducible code and raw inputs.

Secondary summaries remain useful when the primary source is unavailable, but must stay labelled as secondary.

## Do not promote by rhetoric

The following are not promotion gates:

```text
famous witness
military/government title
large audience
many retellings
many AI agents
mysterious missing document
classified status
one failed conventional explanation
one surviving anomaly
```

## Predictions

Future predictions must be frozen before their deadline with explicit success and failure criteria. Failed predictions must remain visible and may lower confidence.

## Corrections

Do not rewrite old receipts to hide mistakes. Add a correction or superseding version and preserve the prior artifact.

## Safety and privacy

Do not publish private personal data, doxxing material, unsafe instructions or claims presented as medical/legal certainty. Preserve evidence without turning living people into targets of an investigation.

## Best contribution types

TOPA especially values:

- `PRIMARY_SOURCE_RECOVERY`
- `CLOCK_OR_TIME_STANDARD_CORRECTION`
- `SENSOR_LIMIT_ANALYSIS`
- `GEOMETRY_RECONSTRUCTION`
- `SOURCE_INDEPENDENCE_GRAPH`
- `CONVENTIONAL_MODEL_THAT_FULLY_FITS`
- `CONVENTIONAL_MODEL_FALSIFICATION`
- `EXTRAORDINARY_MODEL_FALSIFICATION`
- `FAILED_PREDICTION_RECEIPT`
- `REPRODUCIBLE_RAW_DATA_REPLAY`
- `NEGATIVE_RESULT`

A clean `UNRESOLVED` is better than a forced answer.
